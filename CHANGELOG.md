# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-03

The visual generator now starts from one coherent art direction. This revision addresses the near-white/near-black canvases and independently combined decorative graphics in 0.1.

- Twelve chromatic canvas families, continuous OKLCH colour generation and direction-specific colour relationships. Rich colour is the default; neutral grounds remain an explicit option.
- Eight inspected visual grammars with compatible typography, spatial rules, material and graphic purpose. Eleven structured graphic operators draw a single subject or one disciplined repeat system. Automatic rotated/mirrored translucent copies are removed.
- Botanical leaves follow one curved stem with tapered shapes and veins. Axonometric ribs, rails and dimensions share one projection. Woven graphics use related yarn colours, warp/weft directions and a selvedge. Signs prioritize type, border and a small ornament.
- New website compositions and six-slide PowerPoint specimens share the same saved specification. Ten bilingual PPTX examples retain editable text, tables, charts and embedded chart data.
- An eight-direction website catalogue, updated screenshots, bilingual documentation and an updated portable Skill. The Skill requires subject-relevant graphics and rendered review when adapting themes to real projects.
- Regression checks measure actual canvas chroma/lightness and grammar compatibility. Browser checks cover all 16 supported direction/layout pairs in two languages at desktop/mobile widths.
- Algorithm `random-design/2` and separate version-2 history storage. Old specifications and histories retain their matching 0.1 release. Recent similarity defaults are explicitly 40 shown themes, minimum attribute distance 0.22 and a 1,400-candidate budget. Infeasible requests still fail without silent relaxation.

These are curated grammars with generated variants. Variant counts and fingerprint differences do not establish independent art styles, perceptual uniqueness or aesthetic quality.
